# AVINASH STUDIO — BRAND GUIDE & DESIGN SYSTEM

## Brand Essence
> **"Modern Digital Craft with a Desi Soul."**

### Core Pillars
- **75% Editorial Minimalism / 25% Expressive Indian Motifs:** Archival paper textures, stamps, vintage borders, and Devanagari accents without falling into generic cliches.
- **Micro-interactions:** Procedural WebAudio click and hover feedback, spring cursor tracking.
- **Typography:** Serif display headings paired with high-legibility clean modern sans body typography and Devanagari scripts.
